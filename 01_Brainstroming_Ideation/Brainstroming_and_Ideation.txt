EDUGENIE – GOOGLE GEMINI POWERED LEARNING ASSISTANT

PHASE 1 – BRAINSTORMING & IDEATION

1. Problem Statement

Students often spend a lot of time searching for explanations, study resources, summaries, and practice questions from different sources. Complex topics may also be difficult to understand through traditional learning methods. There is a need for a simple and interactive learning assistant that can provide quick, understandable, and personalized educational support.

2. Proposed Solution

EduGenie is a Google Gemini-powered learning assistant designed to help students learn more effectively through an easy-to-use web application. It uses generative AI to answer questions, explain difficult concepts in simple language, generate quizzes, provide summaries, and guide students with useful learning resources.

3. Objectives

• Provide quick answers to students' academic questions.
• Explain complex topics in simple and understandable language.
• Generate personalized quizzes for practice.
• Provide concise and useful summaries of topics.
• Help students follow suitable learning paths.
• Provide useful learning resources and guidance.
• Create a simple and user-friendly learning experience.

4. Target Users

• School students
• College students
• Students preparing for exams
• Self-learners
• Learners who need quick explanations and practice

5. Key Features

• AI-powered Question & Answer
• Simple Explanation of Complex Topics
• Personalized Quiz Generation
• Topic Summarization
• Smart Learning Paths
• Learning Resource Guidance
• User-friendly Web Interface
